Counts every call you have locked, whether or not the round has settled yet.

How points are given

  • +10 — called a settled round correctly
  • +15 — called Zoomies correctly (it beat the chain's 40-block target)
  • +5 — bonus on every hit from a streak of 3 or more
  • 0 — a miss never subtracts. Call again.

Grading uses completedBlockCount on the settled round — the same rule the call itself was locked against, so the score cannot disagree with the chain.
