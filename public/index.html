How this uses Squeekchain

  • Reads /v1/status for live wheel telemetry (core status, RPM, block height, work per 1000 blocks).
  • Reads /v1/settlements and /v1/core for settled rounds, difficulty and the round schedule.
  • Verifies settled rounds against SqueekCore 0xfd6c166d4b8e2962e65746a13e5a0797cd4de0c4 on Sepolia (chain 11155111), linking each result to its settlement transaction.
  • Your call is locked with a plain wallet signature (EIP-191, zero gas, no private key ever leaves your wallet) and kept locally against the round number.
  • Optional: send a zero-value SQK approve on Sepolia to stamp your call on-chain.

Independent community project. Not affiliated with the Squeekchain team.
