How to play

Free to play, no entry fee. No account, no deposit, nothing to sign up for. Read the live wheel, call the round before it closes, and the chain grades you.

  1. 01

    Watch the wheel, not a clock

    Squeekchain's proof of work is a real hamster wheel. A Raspberry Pi RP2040 counts every turn with an LM393 optical sensor and reports it to the core. When the wheel turns, the core does work; when it stops, the chain goes idle. Everything on this page — rpm, distance, blocks — is that sensor, straight off api.squeekchain.com.

  2. 02

    Work gets packaged into blocks

    Every 1,000,000,000 work units seals one block. The core aims for 40 blocks in a round — that number is on the chain itself, and this page reads it live, so if the core ever retunes, the game retunes with it.

  3. 03

    The round runs 10 minutes

    Rounds are 600 seconds, fixed by the core contract on Sepolia — not something this site decides. Round numbers start from the chain's genesis and every settled round is written to Sepolia with a transaction hash you can open and check yourself.

  4. 04

    Call it before it closes

    Pick Nap, Jog or Zoomies and lock it in with a free wallet signature. When the round settles, the chain's own block count decides what you called. One call per round — no edits, no takebacks.

How this uses Squeekchain

  • Reads /v1/status for live wheel telemetry — core status, wheel speed, distance, block height and work per 1000 blocks.
  • Reads /v1/core and /v1/settlements for round schedule, difficulty and every settled round, each linked to its settlement transaction.
  • Cross-checks settled rounds against SqueekCore 0xfd6c166d4b8e2962e65746a13e5a0797cd4de0c4 on Sepolia (chain 11155111).
  • Your call is locked with a plain wallet signature (EIP-191, zero gas, no private key ever leaves your wallet).
  • Nothing is minted, bought or sold here. There is no token to buy and no money to lose.
